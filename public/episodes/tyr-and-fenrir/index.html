Norse myth

The God Who Sacrificed His Hand to Save the World

The gods bind the wolf Fenrir with a ribbon made of six impossible things. Týr pays the price.

About this episode

In Norse mythology, one god was willing to put his hand inside the mouth of the most dangerous wolf in the gods' world. But Tyr knew there was only one way to prove that Fenrir could be trusted.

As the gods sought to bind the growing wolf, they turned to Gleipnir — a magical binding forged by the dwarfs. Fenrir agreed to test it, but only if one of the gods placed a hand in his jaws as a guarantee. Tyr accepted.

What followed became one of the most famous and tragic moments in Norse mythology — the sacrifice that cost Tyr his hand and left Fenrir bound.
