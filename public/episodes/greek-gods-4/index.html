Greek Gods · Part 4

Hephaestus, Aphrodite & Ares: The Net That Caught the God of War

Hera threw her lame son off Olympus. He answered with a golden throne, and later with chains too fine for any god to see.

About this episode

Hera threw her own son off Olympus because he was born lame. A later tradition says he answered with a golden throne that trapped her the moment she sat down.

In this part of our Greek Gods series, Hephaestus grows up in a sea cave, becomes the craftsman Olympus cannot do without, and marries Aphrodite. But Aphrodite loves Ares, the god of war, and Homer's Odyssey tells what happened when the Sun saw them together: Hephaestus forged chains too fine for any god to see.

The net falls, the gods crowd in to look, and one of them says he would gladly take the chains himself. Only Poseidon does not laugh. Hesiod gives Aphrodite and Ares three children: Fear, Panic, and Harmony.

This is Part 4 of our Greek Gods series. Next: all twelve gods of the series in one story.
