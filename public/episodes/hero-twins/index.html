Maya myth · the Popol Vuh

Hunahpu and Xbalanque Went Down to the Maya Underworld, Where Their Father Died

The lords of death invite two brothers to a ball game. It is the trap that killed their father, and the brothers know it.

About this episode

The lords of the Maya underworld invited two brothers to a ball game. It was a trap, and the brothers knew it, because it was how their father had died.

This is the story of Hunahpu and Xbalanque, the hero twins of the Popol Vuh, the book of the K'iche' Maya of Guatemala. It was written down in the 1550s, after the Spanish conquest, and copied by a priest around 1701. The twins go down to Xibalba, the underworld, and face the same lords who killed their father, Hun Hunahpu. They cross rivers of blood on their blowguns, greet every lord by name, spend the nights in the House of Darkness, the House of Cold and the House of Bats, and win the ball game with a rabbit, a turtle and two canes growing in their grandmother's house. The lords of death, it turns out, could not resist a good show.

No one knows how old the story is. Maya vases from a thousand years before the book show figures that may be the twins, but the book itself was written after the conquest, and scholars still argue about what it kept.
